# Cabo

Cabo, the memory card game, for 2–6 players on their phones. Built with Python and Flask, and runs in Docker.

## Build & run

```bash
./build.sh            # build the image and start/replace the container
./build.sh --fresh    # rebuild from scratch
```

Then open `http://<server>:8088/`.

* **Lobby** `/t/main/`: share this link. Each phone taps a free seat (1–5) and enters a name.
* **Start game**: any seated player can start once 2+ are seated.
* **Table view** `/t/main/board`: an optional TV/laptop screen. Public info only: card backs, piles, log, scores.
* **Private tables**: use any name, e.g. `/t/friday-night/`.

## Rules (our local rules)

These are based on the original Cabo, with our house rules on top.
The in-game **Rules** button shows the same summary.

* **Deal:** 4 face-down cards each, and no card is turned up (the discard pile starts empty).
  Each player then looks at their cards 1 and 4 (fixed, not chosen) for a few seconds.
* **On your turn, do one of these:**
  * **Draw from the deck** (only you see it), then either keep it by swapping out one of your cards,
    or discard it. Discarding it does nothing else, even if it's a power card.
  * **Take the top discard** and swap it for one of your cards.
  * **Call CABO** instead of drawing. Everyone else gets one more turn, then the round ends.
* **Matching:** when keeping a card you may swap out 2–4 cards of the *same rank* for it.
  If they don't all match, everyone sees them, they go back, the new card is discarded and your turn ends.
* **Powers:** trigger only when you **swap a power card out of your hand**, whether you're keeping a deck or
  discard-pile card. A drawn power card that's discarded does nothing. Powers are optional, and a matched set gives one power.
  * 7/8 **Peek:** look at one of your own cards.
  * 9/10 **Spy:** look at another player's card.
  * J **Swap:** blind-swap any two cards (yours or anyone's).
  * Q **Peek & swap:** look at any card, then you *may* blind-swap any two cards (not necessarily that one).
  * Joker: **give** one of your cards to another player, or make another player **draw** a card
    from the deck into their hand (face down, unseen).
  * A♠ **Turn over:** turn up to 2 cards (anyone's) face up for the rest of the round.
* **Values:** A 1, 2–10 face value, J / Q / black K 10, **red K −2**, **Joker 11**.
* **End:** a round only ends when CABO is called. The caller gets **−1** on their final count, then the lowest total wins.
  **Ties:** the tied player with the most cards wins; if still tied, it's a draw between them.
  Each game is a single round; the table keeps a 🏆 tally of rounds won.
* **SLAPDOWN:** when a card is discarded, **everyone** (the discarder too) can race to slap a card of the same rank
  onto it (tap SLAP!, then the card). The window is 5 s and the fastest slap wins. Slaps make pairs: the first slap is
  one card, and after that every slap must be a pair. **Any slap can steal** cards from other players' hands (e.g. ones
  you've spied), and a pair can even be two stolen cards. After each good steal, the stealer must give that victim one of their cards or make them draw from
  the deck (15 s to choose, otherwise the victim draws). A wrong slap is shown to everyone, the cards stay with their
  owners, and the slapper draws 1 card blind. Slapped cards don't trigger powers.
* **Quartet:** on your turn, play all four cards of one rank. That's an immediate win, whatever else you hold.
* When the deck runs out, the whole discard pile is shuffled and becomes a fresh deck.

Every rule is a setting in `RULES` at the top of `app/game.py`.

## Reverse proxy

The app trusts `X-Forwarded-*` headers (`PROXY_HOPS` in compose).
For a sub-path, send `X-Forwarded-Prefix`:

```nginx
location /cabo/ {
    proxy_pass         http://<server>:8088/;
    proxy_set_header   Host              $host;
    proxy_set_header   X-Forwarded-For   $proxy_add_x_forwarded_for;
    proxy_set_header   X-Forwarded-Proto $scheme;
    proxy_set_header   X-Forwarded-Prefix /cabo;
    proxy_read_timeout 60s;     # phones long-poll for ~20s at a time
}
```

## Layout

```
app/game.py             Rules engine + RULES settings
app/app.py              Flask routes, JSON API (/api/state long-poll, /api/act moves)
app/templates/          lobby / seat (phone) / board (table) / _rules
app/static/js/          common.js, seat.js, board.js
app/static/cards/       54 faces + back.svg (generated by tools/generate_cards.py)
build.sh                build + run, only touches the cabo container/image
```

## Notes

* **Version / cache busting:** `app/version.txt` holds a single number that's bumped on every change.
  It's appended to every JS/CSS/card URL (`?v=7`), so phones fetch fresh files after an update.
  The running version is shown at the bottom of the in-game Rules screen.
* **Secrets:** nothing secret lives in the repo. Put real values in a `.env` file next to `docker-compose.yml`
  (copy `.env.example`). `.env` is ignored by git and by the Docker build. If `CABO_SECRET_KEY` isn't set,
  the app generates a random key at startup.
* State lives in memory, so there's one gunicorn worker (32 threads) and a restart clears the tables.
* A phone only ever receives its own private info: what it drew, and peeks/spies while they're on screen.
  The app doesn't remember your cards for you. That's the game!
