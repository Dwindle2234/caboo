How to play Cabo

Goal: have the lowest total when someone calls Cabo. You can't see your own cards, so you have to remember them.

Setup

Everyone gets {{ RULES.cards_per_player }} cards face down in a row. No card is turned up, so the discard pile starts empty. Before play, each player looks at cards {% for i in RULES.initial_peek_slots %}{{ i + 1 }}{% if not loop.last %} and {% endif %}{% endfor %} (the first and last) for a few seconds. Remember them!

Your turn — do one of these

  • Draw from the deck. Only you see it. Then either:
    • Keep it: tap one of your cards to swap it out (that card goes face-up on the discard pile), or
    • Discard it. Nothing else happens.
  • Take the top discard and swap it for one of your cards.
  • Call CABO instead of drawing. Everyone else gets one more turn, then the cards are shown.

Matching

When keeping a card you can swap out 2, 3 or 4 of your cards at once if they're all the same rank. They're all replaced by the one new card. Get it wrong and everyone sees the cards, they go back, the new card is discarded and you lose your turn.

SLAPDOWN!

Whenever a card lands on the discard pile, everyone, including the player who discarded it, can race to slap a card of the same rank onto it (e.g. a 10 on a 10). Tap SLAP!, then tap your card. The window lasts {{ RULES.slap_seconds }} seconds, and the fastest slap wins.

  • Slaps make pairs. The first slap is one card, which makes a pair with the discard. After that, every slap must be a pair.
  • Stealing: any slap can use cards from other players' hands. Tap their card instead of yours. Spied a 10 in Tom's hand? Slap it onto the 10! When a pair is needed, it can be two of yours, one of each, or two stolen cards.
  • Pay back a steal: after a successful steal you must either give the player one of your cards or make them draw one from the deck. You have {{ RULES.steal_choice_seconds }} seconds to choose, or they draw. Steal two cards and you pay back twice.
  • Wrong slap (e.g. a 9 on a 10, or stealing a card that isn't a 10): everyone sees the card(s), they stay with their owners, and you draw {{ RULES.slap_penalty_cards }} card blind as a penalty.
  • Slapped cards don't trigger powers.

Quartet

Hold all four cards of one rank? On your turn, instead of drawing, tap Quartet and play all four. That's an immediate win, whatever other cards you're still holding and whatever the points. Get it wrong and everyone sees the cards, they go back and you lose your turn.

Power cards

A power works when you swap a power card OUT of your hand (by keeping a card from the deck or the discard pile). Discarding a power card you've just drawn does nothing. Using the power is optional. If you swap out a matching set, you get the power once.

  • 7 · 8 — Peek: look at one of your own cards.
  • 9 · 10 — Spy: look at one card of another player.
  • J — Swap: blind-swap any two cards (yours or anyone's). Nobody looks.
  • Q — Peek & swap: look at any card, then you may blind-swap any two cards. It doesn't have to be the one you looked at.
  • Joker: give one of your cards to another player, or pick a player who must draw a card from the deck into their hand (face down, nobody sees it).
  • A♠ — Turn over: turn up to {{ RULES.turnover_max }} cards (yours or anyone's) face up for the rest of the round.

Empty deck

When the deck runs out, the whole discard pile is shuffled and becomes a fresh deck.

Card values

A 1 · 2–10 face value · J, Q and black K 10 · red K −2 · Joker 11

End of the round

  • The round only ends when someone calls CABO and everyone else has had one more turn (or when someone plays a quartet). The last discard can still be slapped before the cards are shown.
  • Lowest total wins.
  • Ties: the tied player with the most cards wins. Still tied on cards? It's a draw between them.
  • Each game is a single round. Deal again for another.
